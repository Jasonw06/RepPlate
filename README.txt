REPPLATE
Free workout guide + calorie counter that runs as a web app on your phone.

WHAT IT DOES
- Train: 6 starter routines (home, core, dumbbells, gym push / pull / legs) you can edit,
  plus a library of 876 exercises, each with a start/finish demo and step-by-step instructions.
- Coach mode (hands-free): "3, 2, 1, go", then the demo moves in time with each rep while
  the phone's built-in voice counts out loud. At the end of the set it says "Rest, 45 seconds",
  counts down the last seconds, announces the next set or exercise and starts it by itself,
  until the workout is done. Pause any time. Rep speed (slow / normal / fast), the voice and
  "I tap when done" mode are under Me > Workout coach. The voice is free and works offline.
- Real video if you want it: on any exercise page, paste a YouTube link (Add a video). In
  workout mode it plays on repeat with the sound off while the coach counts.
- Workout mode also logs your reps and weight, runs the rest timer (beeps and vibrates), and
  keeps the screen awake. Timed holds like planks get a countdown.
- Food: scan a barcode, or search over 6,000 everyday foods built into the app (works offline,
  with portions like "1 medium banana"). Choose how much you ate and it works out energy,
  protein, carbs and fat. Quick add, your own foods, and one-tap "add again". Branded products
  can also be searched online through Open Food Facts when their search is available.
- Snap a meal: take a photo of your plate; Google's free Gemini AI lists the foods with estimated
  grams and calories, you check/adjust them, then log. Needs a free key from
  aistudio.google.com/apikey (Me > Photo & voice logging). Free daily limits apply, and Google may
  use free-tier photos/text to improve its products. It asks Google's fast Flash-Lite model first
  and moves on to another free model if Google is slow or overloaded, so you're never left waiting
  for more than about a minute. Me > Check it works sends a real test and says how fast it answered.
- Say what you ate: tap the mic (or type) "two eggs and toast" and it fills in the foods. Works
  with the free AI, or a basic built-in version without it.
- Same as yesterday: one tap repeats yesterday's breakfast/lunch/dinner. Save as a meal: keep
  meals you eat often and log them with one tap from Add food.
- Home-screen shortcuts (Android): press and hold the RepPlate icon for Snap a meal, Say what
  you ate, Scan a barcode and Start a workout.
- Meal ideas: a sample day of everyday South African meals (oats, pap, maas, pilchards, samp &
  beans, chicken, mince, boerewors...) sized to your calorie goal. Swap any meal for another
  option and log a meal with one tap. A guideline, not medical advice.
- Food needs (Me > Food needs): allergies and intolerances (lactose, milk/dairy, eggs, peanuts,
  tree nuts, gluten/wheat, soya, fish, shellfish, sesame), how you eat (everything, pescatarian,
  vegetarian, vegan), halaal / no pork, no beef, and any other foods you don't eat. Meal ideas,
  the week plan, the shopping list and "What can I eat?" leave those foods out or swap them
  (lactose-free or soya milk, gluten-free bread, rice cakes, halaal boerewors, chicken or soya
  mince) and say what was changed. Scanned products show a warning from their allergen list,
  and searched foods get a warning badge going by their name. Always read the label: RepPlate
  can't promise a food is safe for a serious allergy.
- Your usuals: Today shows the meal you usually eat at this time of day and the foods you eat
  most. One tap logs it.
- What can I eat now?: tap it and get 3 ideas that fit the calories and protein you have left
  today (with the free AI, or from the built-in meals without it). Tap one to log it.
- Plan my week: plans 7 days of meals with no repeats from one day to the next, then makes a
  shopping list grouped by shop aisle, with amounts. Tick items off and copy the list.
- Read a nutrition label: in Create food, take a photo of the label and the free AI fills in the
  numbers (kJ is turned into kcal for you).
- Weight tracker: weigh in from Today in two taps. Progress shows a chart, your trend, and a
  smart goal that checks what you ate against how your weight moved and suggests a better
  calorie goal.
- Workout schedule: pick a routine for each day of the week. Today shows today's workout or
  "Rest day". During a workout it shows what you did last time and suggests when to go heavier,
  and the summary lists new personal bests.
- Quick workout: pick your time, equipment and focus and it builds a workout. Swap any exercise
  for a similar one (in the workout or in the routine editor).
- Water, streaks and a weekly summary: tap + for each glass, a streak badge for days in a row
  you logged food, and a Monday summary of last week.
- Look and feel: built like Apple's own iPhone apps (Health, Fitness, App Store). Plain cards on the
  iOS grouped background, Apple's iOS 26 system colours and text sizes, the iPhone system font with
  rounded numbers, true-black dark mode, a floating Liquid Glass tab bar with a separate round scan
  button, a large title that moves into the top bar as you scroll, pages that slide in and out,
  sheets you can pull down to close, and (from the home screen) swipe in from the left edge to go
  back. "Reduce Transparency" on the phone makes the glass solid.
- Workouts: plate calculator and warm-up sets for barbell lifts; supersets and drop sets (set them
  in the routine editor); rate how hard each set felt (RPE 6–10) and the "try heavier" tip uses
  it; six ready-made programs (Beginner Full Body, 5×5 Strength, Push/Pull/Legs 6 or 3 days, Home
  Fat Burn, Dumbbell Builder) that fill your week and alternate A/B workouts; an interval timer
  for Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP and custom intervals with the voice coach.
- Activity and calories burned: workouts, cardio (walk, run, cycle, swim, sport…) and steps you
  type in. Off / Half / All of it is added to what you can eat today. GPS tracking for walks, runs,
  hikes and rides while the app is open.
- Fasting timer: 12:12 up to OMAD, a live timer on Today, history and streaks.
- Recipes: add the ingredients of something you cook (or paste the recipe for the free AI to
  read) and log a serving with one tap.
- Nutrient detail on the Food tab (fibre, sugar, saturated fat, salt against healthy limits) and
  Nutri-Score A–E grades on scanned products.
- Progress: body measurements with a body-fat estimate (US Navy formula), private progress photos
  with before/after compare (kept only on the phone, not in backups), a Strength page with a
  workout calendar, sets per week and estimated 1-rep max for every lift, and a muscle recovery map
  on Train.
- Export your food log, workouts and body data as spreadsheet (CSV) files, and add your workout
  days, a daily food reminder and a weekly weigh-in to your phone's calendar (Me > Export).
- AI coach chat (with the free key): ask about your eating and training and it answers from your
  own logs, goals and food needs.
- Macro styles (balanced, high protein, low carb, keto) and daily habits with streaks.
- Works on every phone size, from small phones (320 px wide) to large ones; longer button
  labels switch to short ones on narrow screens.
- Goals in kcal or kJ, a goal estimator, a 7-day chart, workout history, and backup/restore.
- Works offline once it has been opened (food lookups still need internet).

PUT IT ONLINE FOR FREE (needed so the camera works on your phone)
The phone camera only works on a secure (https) web address, so the folder has to be hosted.
Two free options:

  Option A - Netlify Drop (easiest)
  1. Unzip this folder on a computer.
  2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
  3. Drag the whole "repplate" folder onto the page.
  4. Netlify gives you a link like https://something.netlify.app
     Make a free account when it asks, so the site stays up. You can rename the link in
     Site settings.

  Option B - GitHub Pages
  1. Make a free GitHub account and a new public repository.
  2. Upload everything inside this folder (Add file > Upload files).
  3. Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root) > Save.
  4. Your app appears at https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/REPO-NAME/ after a minute or two.

PUT IT ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
- iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
- Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
The first time you scan, allow camera access.

YOUR DATA
Everything is saved on the phone itself; there are no accounts. Progress photos stay on the phone and are not in backups. Only barcode numbers and
food searches are sent to Open Food Facts. If you set up the free AI, the photos and text you
choose to send go to Google Gemini; your key stays on the phone and is left out of backups. Use Me > Save backup now and then, and
Me > Restore on a new phone.

CHANGING THINGS LATER
- Starter routines live near the top of app.js (STARTERS). Colours and fonts are in styles.css.
- After uploading changed files, open sw.js and change VERSION (for example 'repplate-v2')
  so phones pick up the new version.
- Opening index.html straight from your computer won't load the exercise list; it has to be
  served from a web address (any of the hosts above, or a local web server).
